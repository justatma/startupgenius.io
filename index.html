About the Author

ATMA DEGEYNDT

Atma’s journey through the realms of marketing, psychology, and entrepreneurship is as unconventional as it is inspiring. With over two decades of hands-on experience, he has harnessed the power of digital marketing and product management to propel companies to remarkable growth, exemplified by his pivotal role in scaling an e-commerce company from zero to $1.8 million in annual sales within 18 months.

In the vibrant landscape of New York, Atma has established himself as a philosopher in practice and a maven in marketing, currently leading as the Chief Marketing Officer for the cutting-edge marketing agency Growth Bastards. His academic pursuits—spanning Journalism and Mass Communications, Educational Psychology, and a dreamt Ph.D. in Marketing Psychology—have shaped him into a thinker with a rare blend of analytical rigor and creative excellence.

Atma’s narrative, however, extends beyond his professional achievements. His life has been deeply influenced by the wisdom of swamis and yogis, igniting a lifelong dedication to Eastern esotericism. This spiritual grounding, balanced with profound studies in Western thought, has endowed him with a unique lens for understanding personal and organizational transformation.
Thanks to the foundational support from corporations like WIX.com and WeWork, he has been a catalyst for hundreds of startups. These experiences honed his expertise and sowed the seeds for “Startup Genius,” his comprehensive guide for entrepreneurs.
His role as a global yoga teacher trainer further accentuates his commitment to merging ancient wisdom with modern didactics. Coupled with his expertise as a facial analytics expert and instructional design specialist, Atma embodies the essence of a modern-day Renaissance man.

Atma’s academic endeavors are as diverse as his professional ones. Having pursued undergraduate degrees in Journalism and Mass Communications, graduate studies in Educational Psychology, and still dreaming of a Ph.D. in Marketing Psychology, he embodies a blend of analytical acumen and creative thought. Multilingual, speaking English, Spanish, and French, Atma’s global perspective is as broad as it is deep.

In the heart of one of the world’s most dynamic cities, Atma also serves those around him as a volunteer New York State EMT and a certified CPR teacher, where his passion for saving lives unfolds in the less visible yet equally impactful arena of emergency medical services. This dedication to service is an aspect of his character that, while not at the forefront of his professional identity, is an essential part of his commitment to the well-being of others.

With an approach that challenges conventional wisdom, Atma’s upcoming translation of the Yoga Sutras of Patañjali and his thought-provoking book, “Startup Genius,” are testaments to his desire to inspire and initiate change. Atma is not just an author; he is an experiential philosopher calling others to explore the depths of their potential and to enact the change they wish to see in the business world and beyond.
